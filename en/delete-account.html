Delete your account

In the app (immediate)

Settings → Delete account → confirm. This cannot be undone.

By email

Write to andre.f.pacheco@gmail.com with the name you use in the app. We ask for confirmation from the same Apple or Google account and delete within 7 days.

What is deleted

Name, answers, times, rankings, mind profile, history, notification token and the link to your Apple or Google account. Progress stored on the device is removed when you uninstall.

What is kept

Purchase identifiers for the 5-year tax period, unlinked from the name. Technical logs expire in 14 days.
