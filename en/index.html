How it works

Daily challengeSeven puzzles, one per aspect of the mind, the same for everyone. Your time goes to the day's ranking; the fastest is King of the Day.
Mind profileA seven-axis radar that grows with the levels you beat and your times. Shows your strongest side and what grew most.
Endless levelsEvery puzzle type has a never-ending trail, the same for everyone, with milestones and best times.
Hints that teachEvery hint explains the deduction. Every puzzle has a single solution and can be solved by reasoning, never by guessing.

The puzzles

Queens, Thread, Tango, Towers, Target Sum, Bridges, Fit, Pairs, Sequence, Hidden Map, Spot the Difference, Letters and the Lightning mode.
