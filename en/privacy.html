Privacy Policy — BrainIAC

Last updated: October 12, 2026 (pre-release)

Controller: André Pacheco, Brasília, DF, Brazil — andre.f.pacheco@gmail.com

The app has not launched yet. This policy describes the data handling planned for the first version and will be updated before store publication.

Why an account

Training and levels work without an account, with progress stored on the device. The daily challenge with ranking, King of the Day, the synced mind profile and a purchase valid on both stores require signing in with Apple or Google.

We do not collect email, phone, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers. No ads, no cross-app tracking, no data sales. The app measures performance in games; it does not diagnose or promise any health effect.

Where data lives

Amazon Web Services servers in the United States (us-east-1), encrypted at rest and in transit, under AWS contractual safeguards for international transfer. Only the developer accesses the data, to operate the app and answer requests.

Your rights (GDPR, LGPD, CCPA and local laws)

You can access, correct or delete your data at any time, in the app (Settings) or through the Delete account page.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
